Current derived scene removes the cassette points while retaining the surrounding Gaussian scene. The rigid cassette GLB is refined from the supplied model. Back and side surfaces are visual approximations; this is not an official extracted game model or a new Gaussian training result.

GPU captures share the live camera: 181 approach frames and 240 orbit frames over 16 seconds (15 distinct orbit images per second). The 3414x1440 and 2560x1080 AVIF variants derive from already-lossy WebP Q90; AVIF quality50 is not a lossless master. Runtime RAF frequency is not source image FPS.

The same rigid object, projected hull, exterior halo with protected interior, separate presentation/input handoff clocks and bounded frame caches are reused in the current public example and CVA adaptation.
